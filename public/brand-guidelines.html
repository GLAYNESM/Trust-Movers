Brand Guidelines

Last updated: July 2026

Using the Trust Name & Mark

The Trust name, logo, and the visual style of this site are the property of Trust. These guidelines exist so the brand stays consistent wherever it appears — and so it's clear what is and isn't okay to do with it.

Logo

The mark pairs a red truck-and-motion icon with the wordmark "Trust." Please keep the icon and wordmark in their original proportions, and give the mark reasonable clear space around it — don't stretch, recolor, rotate, or overlay text on top of it.

Color Palette

Red — #C1121F
Red Light — #E63946
Black — #0A0A0A
Dark — #111111
Muted — #888888

Typography

Barlow Condensed (bold, uppercase, tight letter-spacing) is used for headings and anything that needs to feel confident and direct. Barlow (regular weight) is used for body copy and everything meant to be read comfortably.

Voice

Direct, calm, and competent. We talk about moving the way a good crew actually behaves on the day — clear, on time, no surprises.

What Not To Do

  • Don't use the logo to imply a partnership or endorsement that doesn't exist.
  • Don't recreate the mark from scratch — use the official files.
  • Don't pair the red against clashing background colors outside this palette.
