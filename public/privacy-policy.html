Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What This Covers

This policy explains what information Trust collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and how you can control it. It applies to trustmovers.com (or wherever this site is hosted) and the services described on it.

Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to actually run the business and respond to you:

  • Quote requests: name, email, phone, moving dates, pickup/destination ZIP codes, home size, and any special items you mention.
  • Account information: if you register, we store your name, email, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Chat messages: messages you send through the on-site chat widget, along with the name you provide.
  • Basic technical data: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) used only for security and troubleshooting.

How We Use It

Your information is used to prepare moving estimates, respond to enquiries, manage your account, and improve the site. We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we don't use it for advertising.

Email & WhatsApp

If you request a quote and provide an email address, we'll send your estimate there. If you use the chat widget, your message may be forwarded to our WhatsApp number so a team member can follow up with you directly.

How Long We Keep It

Quote requests and chat messages are kept for as long as reasonably needed to follow up with you and for our own record-keeping, after which they may be archived or deleted. Account information is kept until you ask us to delete it.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by reaching out through the contact details on this site. If you created an account, you can also stop using it whenever you like.

Security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text, and access to admin data is restricted to authorized team members. No online system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, standard precautions to protect your information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this page from time to time as the business or the site changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the contact details on our homepage and we'll be glad to help.
