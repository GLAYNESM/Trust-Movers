Our Approach to Cookies

We keep this simple: this site does not use advertising cookies, third-party ad trackers, or sell your browsing data to anyone. Here's exactly what does run in your browser when you visit.

What We Actually Use

Local Storage (not a cookie, but similar)

When you log in, we save a login token in your browser's local storage so you don't have to log in again every time you visit. This stays on your device and is only sent to our own server — never to a third party.

Essential Session Data

Basic technical data needed to keep the site functioning securely (like recognizing an active admin session). Nothing here is used for tracking you across other websites.

What We Don't Use

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies
  • No third-party analytics that track you across other sites
  • No cookie-based cross-site fingerprinting

Managing Your Browser Storage

You're always free to clear your browser's local storage and cookies through your browser's settings — doing so will simply log you out of any account you had open here.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, reach out through the contact details on our homepage.
