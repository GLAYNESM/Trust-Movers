Your Rights

Last updated: July 2026

As a Trust Customer, You Have the Right To:

A Clear, Written Estimate

Before your move, you're entitled to a clear estimate covering the services, timing, and cost involved — not a vague verbal promise.

Ask Questions Before You Commit

You can ask about our process, our crew, insurance/liability coverage, and anything else before agreeing to book — and we'll answer honestly.

Cancel or Reschedule

Plans change. You have the right to request a cancellation or reschedule — reach out as early as you can so we can accommodate you.

Raise a Concern

If something about your move didn't go as planned, you have the right to raise it with us directly and have it looked into properly, not brushed aside.

Control Over Your Data

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted — see our Privacy Policy for details.

How to Reach Us

If any of the above isn't happening, or you just have a question about your rights as a customer, contact us through the details on our homepage — we want to know if something's gone wrong so we can fix it.
